# Move sound

`move.mp3` is one knock cut from "Chess Pieces Move (Close)" by JJTaynos:
https://freesound.org/people/JJTaynos/sounds/733927/

License: Creative Commons 0 (public domain). No attribution is required; it is credited here anyway.

Taken from the high-quality preview (48 kHz mono MP3), the knock at 35.685 s:
a 0.35 s cut starting 3 ms before the impact, high-passed at 80 Hz to remove room
rumble, faded out over the last 0.15 s, peak-normalised to −1 dBFS, and encoded
as 96 kbps mono MP3. The game varies its pitch and level slightly on every move.

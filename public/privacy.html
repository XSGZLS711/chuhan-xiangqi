隐私说明

更新于 2026 年 11 月 3 日

不登录也能下棋

浏览器里会存一个随机的匿名 Cookie，用来在棋室里认出你。服务器只保存它的哈希值，不知道你是谁。

用 Google 登录时保存什么

  • 只向 Google 请求最基本的资料（openid、email、profile）。
  • 保存：Google 账号编号、Google 上的名字（最多 16 个字）、经 Google 验证的邮箱。邮箱只显示给你本人。
  • 不保存 Google 的访问令牌，也不读取你的通讯录、云盘等任何其他数据。
  • 登录状态保存在一个 HttpOnly Cookie 里，有效期 180 天，退出登录即作废。

棋局数据

  • 好友棋室的棋盘、着法、聊天和你填写的称呼保存在服务器上，供对局双方使用。
  • 本机的「最近对局」和引擎分析结果只保存在你的浏览器里，不会上传。
  • 不投放广告，不使用第三方统计或追踪 Cookie。
  • 网站托管在 Cloudflare，Cloudflare 会按它自己的政策处理访问日志。

删除账号

登录后点右上角的账号，选「删除账号」。账号、登录方式和所有设备上的登录状态会立即删除；棋室记录里只剩一个不再对应任何人的随机编号。

Privacy

Updated 3 November 2026

Without an account, your browser keeps a random anonymous cookie so a game room can recognise you. The server stores only its hash.

With Google sign-in, we ask Google only for basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile) and store your Google account ID, your Google name (up to 16 characters) and your verified email, which is shown only to you. We keep no Google access tokens and read no other Google data. The sign-in cookie is HttpOnly and lasts 180 days, or until you sign out.

Games: friend rooms (board, moves, chat, the name you chose) are stored on the server for both players. Your recent-games list and engine analysis stay in your browser. No ads, no third-party analytics or tracking cookies. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles access logs under its own policy.

Deleting your account: open the account menu (top right) and choose 删除账号 (Delete account). The account, its sign-in method and all sessions are removed at once.

Not intended for children under 13. Questions: GitHub issues.

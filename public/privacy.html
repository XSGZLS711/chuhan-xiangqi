隐私说明

更新于 2026 年 11 月 3 日

不登录也能下棋

浏览器里会存一个随机的匿名 Cookie，用来在棋室里认出你。服务器只保存它的哈希值，不知道你是谁。

用 Google 登录时保存什么

  • 只向 Google 请求最基本的资料（openid、email、profile）。
  • 保存：Google 账号编号、Google 上的名字（最多 32 个字）、经 Google 验证的邮箱。邮箱只显示给你本人。
  • 不保存 Google 的访问令牌，也不读取你的通讯录、云盘等任何其他数据。
  • 登录状态保存在一个 HttpOnly Cookie 里，有效期 180 天，退出登录即作废。

棋局数据

  • 好友棋室的棋盘、着法、聊天和你填写的称呼保存在服务器上，供对局双方使用。
  • 不登录时，「最近对局」只保存在你的浏览器里。
  • 登录后，下完的对局（着法、双方称呼、结果和日期）也保存在服务器上你的账号下，最多保留最近 200 局，换台设备登录也能看到；登录时这个浏览器里已有的对局会一并存进账号。
  • 引擎分析结果只保存在你的浏览器里，不会上传。
  • 不投放广告，不使用第三方统计或追踪 Cookie。
  • 网站托管在 Cloudflare，Cloudflare 会按它自己的政策处理访问日志。

删除账号

登录后点右上角的账号，选「删除账号」。账号、登录方式、存在账号下的对局和所有设备上的登录状态会立即删除；棋室记录里只剩一个不再对应任何人的随机编号。浏览器里保存的「最近对局」不受影响，可以在浏览器设置里清除网站数据。

Privacy

Updated 3 November 2026

Without an account, your browser keeps a random anonymous cookie so a game room can recognise you. The server stores only its hash.

With Google sign-in, we ask Google only for basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile) and store your Google account ID, your Google name (up to 32 characters) and your verified email, which is shown only to you. We keep no Google access tokens and read no other Google data. The sign-in cookie is HttpOnly and lasts 180 days, or until you sign out.

Games: friend rooms (board, moves, chat, the name you chose) are stored on the server for both players. Without an account, your recent-games list stays in your browser. Signed in, your finished games (moves, both names, result and date) are also stored on the server under your account, the latest 200, so you can see them on another device; games already in the browser when you sign in are added too. Engine analysis stays in your browser. No ads, no third-party analytics or tracking cookies. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles access logs under its own policy.

Deleting your account: open the account menu (top right) and choose 删除账号 (Delete account). The account, its sign-in method, the games stored under it and all sessions are removed at once. Games saved in a browser stay there until you clear the site's data.
